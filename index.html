Both live commodity prints this page published yesterday were wrong — one of them by four percent, the other by its sign

At 16:56 yesterday this page published WTI at $75.23, +0.21% as a live Thursday print, described it as “a quiet session” and wrote of “two consecutive flat sessions in the underlying”. Crude SETTLED $78.22, +4.20% on the $75.07 Wednesday settlement, after trading a $74.57–$78.51 range. It was the single largest session the underlying has had in this window and the live print understated it by 3.97%. Gold was published live at $4,271.56, +0.51%; it SETTLED $4,240.33, -0.23%, having run to $4,304.15 and given the lot back. So one live print missed a four-percent rally and the other reported a rise on a day that fell. Two prints, two failures, and the failures were not small. The rule this page wrote one run earlier — that the OIL03 ladder moves on a settled WTI close and never on a SET-session DR close or a live quote — is the only reason that error did not become a third false redraw in a week. Under the old habit the crude print would have argued for marking OIL03 down on a day its underlying rose four percent. That is the second time in three runs that a rule written after an error has caught the next instance of it, and it is a better argument for writing rules down than any amount of being careful.

The macro consequence is that the six-session divergence has broken, and broken in the opposite configuration to the one that ran all week. Gold rose and crude fell for six consecutive settlements; Thursday delivered crude +4.20% and gold -0.23%. Friday’s live prints — crude $78.45, gold $4,266.52 — are doing nothing to it, and they are live prints, which this run has just finished establishing are worth very little. The book’s exposure to the flip is asymmetric and mostly favourable: OIL03 is retired, so the crude rally is watched rather than owned; GOLD19 is the only commodity leg and gold settling lower undercuts the catch-up story behind its spectacular Thursday bar rather than confirming it. PTT gets a real tailwind it did not have yesterday, and this page said so incorrectly at 16:56 on the strength of the bad print. AOT and CK get a cost headwind that reopens two arguments this page had retired for lack of any response in the underlying — reopened as questions, not restored as theses, and neither resolves on an in-progress bar. Baht 33.11, a touch softer, still immaterial.
