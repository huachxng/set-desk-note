The error was not the data — it was reading a mid-session quote as though it were a settled fact while simultaneously arguing at length that settled facts are provisional.

The upgrade is exact and it is not a softening: the revision rule was never about closes, and it was never about DR feeds. It is about unfinished information. Two runs ago the rule was "DR feeds are provisional". Yesterday it widened to "any close is a draft". Today it widens once more and finally lands in the right place — a number is a draft until the session that produced it is over, and that applies to the macro tape a desk note reasons from at least as hard as to the prices it reasons about. The arithmetic test survives intact and is worth restating because it did its job: a break several times the size of the noise is a break, a break the size of the noise is a rumour. What today adds is a prior condition — the number has to be final before the test is worth running at all. That is why this run takes no level decisions on the twelve SET-session names: at 10:25 BKK they have thirty minutes of trade, and thirty minutes is not a session.
